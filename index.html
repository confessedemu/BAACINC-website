About

Michael Brown

Michael Brown, RPP, MCIP, is the founder and principal of BAACINC (Brown & Associates Consulting Inc.). He has over 15 years of planning experience, including ten years at the City of Edmonton (2007–2017), plus senior roles at Dillon Consulting and QuantumPlace. He founded BAACINC in 2018. He helps landowners, non-profits and municipalities in Calgary and Edmonton move housing and development projects from policy to permit.

Informed by planning study and practice in Canada, Italy and the Netherlands.

RPP and MCIP. MSc in Urban Management and Development. More than 15 years in municipal and consulting practice.
