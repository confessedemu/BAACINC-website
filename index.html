About

Michael Brown

Michael Brown, RPP, MCIP, is the founder and principal of BAACINC (Brown & Associates Consulting Inc.). He has over 15 years of planning experience, including eight years at the City of Edmonton, plus senior roles at Dillon Consulting and QuantumPlace. He has consulted independently since 2018 and founded BAACINC in 2024. He helps landowners, non-profits and municipalities in Calgary and Edmonton move housing and development projects from policy to permit.

  • MSc Urban Management and Development, IHS, Erasmus University Rotterdam (2019–21)
  • BA Urban Geography and Art & Design, University of Alberta (2003–07)
  • RPP (APPI) and MCIP, 2014
  • GISP, 2011
  • LEED Green Associate
  • CPTED
  • Urban Design Certificate, SFU, 2015
  • Applied Land Use Planning Certificate, University of Alberta, 2011
  • Public consultation training, 2011
  • Indigenous awareness training, 2017

  • Former lecturer in local economic development, University of Alberta Faculty of Extension
