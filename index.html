Why BAACINC was founded

BAACINC is a boutique planning firm rooted in a passion for places. The founder, Michael Brown, has lived experience in cities big and small across Canada, Italy, and the Netherlands. He is passionate about building strong and caring communities and has urban analysis experience across some 247 cities in 26 countries on 4 continents. Michael is passionate about solution-oriented conversations and is committed to making a positive difference towards environmental, social, and economic progress for customers.

Over 15 years, Michael has contributed to significant projects involving energy retrofits, urban renewal initiatives, environmental stewardship, municipal governance, and economic development initiatives. Michael's networks in urban planning are strong, especially in green building, barrier-free design, affordable housing, community development, data analysis, and real estate development economics in Alberta.

Michael has consulted independently since 2018 and founded BAACINC in 2024 to support a growing interest in triple bottom line projects. Projects have included place making, urban design analysis, affordable housing, energy retrofits, green building, inclusive design, development finance, and advocacy on governance issues. BAACINC is focused on delivering timely and tangible solutions with high environmental, social, and economic returns on investment.

Michael, as Principal, brings well-informed strategic thinking to each project, with over 15 years of planning experience. If you have a project that requires project development analysis, eco-renovation grant coordination, place making, development support, or housing research and analysis, BAACINC can help.

Development Pro Forma

BAACINC prepares development pro formas and project development analysis so you can see whether a proposal holds together before more is spent on design and approvals. The work draws on real estate development economics in Alberta, including development finance and affordable housing.

Housing Analysis

Housing research and analysis, including affordable housing, barrier-free design, inclusive design, and community development. If a project needs housing research and analysis, BAACINC can help frame the question and set out the findings.

Ask about this

Development Permitting

Support for development applications, including secondary suite, subdivision, development planning, and zoning reviews in Calgary and Edmonton. The aim is a complete application and a clear response to the questions that come back.

Ask about this

Energy Retrofit Planning & Grant Coordination

Planning for energy retrofits and eco-renovation grant coordination, with attention to green building. Michael is a LEED Green Associate. The aim is a retrofit path that can be funded and carried out.

Ask about this

Land & Municipal Economic Development Policy

Advice on land and municipal economic development policy: economic action plans, policy planning, and the governance questions that shape where growth goes. This is the focus of Michael's MSc in Urban Management and Development.
